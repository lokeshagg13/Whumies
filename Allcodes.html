Welcome to Whumies!

Born in India in 2025, Whumies began with a single soft toy and a big dream — to make childhood a little softer, a little safer, and a lot more joyful.

As young founders navigating a global team and remote operations, we faced more than a few hiccups (and laughs!). But with each order, we found a rhythm — and a new member in our growing family. Because every smile from you means the world to us.

Our mission? To bring joy, comfort, and creativity into every child’s life through safe, high-quality crochet, plushies, accessories, and playful gift kits — while proudly uplifting India’s handcrafted toy community.
